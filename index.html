Grace's Blender Mega Project

The Stable - an animated short

Finished & premiered by July 2030

A polished, wordless animated short (60-90 seconds), set in a modern stable Grace designs, starring stylised horses. Made in Blender; edited, graded and finished in DaVinci Resolve. Complete before the family leaves for itineration in July 2030 - ready for UCAS (autumn 2031) and every portfolio moment in between.

Guardrails

1Stylised horsesSpirit, not National Geographic. Realism can come later, during A-levels.
2No dialogueVisual storytelling only - and everyone can watch it, in any language.
390 seconds maxFinished and polished beats long and rough. Every time.
4Ship somethingEvery stage ends with a finished artifact, not "progress".
